Escape Act
Unlike Eul's and Wind Waker, a projectile already flying towards the target is not enough. The target receiving Escape Act must already be Nullified when it is cast, in order for Nullify to dispel it.
Omniknight's Repel is marked as dispellable, but Nullifier can't remove it, because Repel also grants Debuff Immunity. Only rare dispels that pierce Debuff Immunity, like Doom's Doom, can remove it.
Natural Buyers
Burst Assassins
Assassins and carries who rely on bursting their target down, but are otherwise easily countered by defensive items and abilities. The classic example is a support buying Ghost Scepter to survive a physical burst. Nullifier provides a ton of bonus damage, which makes heroes with high attack speed a great choice to buy it.
Tips
  1. Linken's Sphere blocks Nullify.
  2. Lotus Orb reflects it back at you, which always forces a decision: is dispelling your target worth dispelling yourself as well? For example, as Troll Warlord you're much less likely to care about Nullifying yourself than as Ursa.
  3. Against illusion heroes, Nullify keeps the real hero identifiable even after they cast their illusions, because the debuff can't be dispelled.
  4. Nullify is one of the few undispellable debuffs in the game. Not even Strong Dispels remove it.
  5. Status Resistance shortens Nullify's duration, but by an insignificant amount. One of the best ways to counter the item is with Debuff Immunity.
  6. Debuff Immunity doesn't remove Nullify, it only prevents it from working. If the immunity runs out before Nullify does, Nullify immediately takes effect as normal.
