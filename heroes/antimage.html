Carry · Mobile · Assassin · Split-pusher

Strengths

  • Highly mobile
  • Can reflect spells
  • Highest base BAT in the game
  • Counters heroes with very high or very low mana pools

Weaknesses

  • Countered by Debuff Immunity
  • Outscaled by late game carries
Game Plan

Early Game

Farm defensively & only join good fights with low to no risk of dying.

Mid Game

Split-push lanes when your team is pushing the opposite side of the map. Or consider showing up for an important objective, but never go into the fight first.

Late Game

Pick off isolated heroes with Blink. Use Mana Void to punish high-mana enemies in team fights.

Extras

Manta, BKB and Counterspell will protect you against most of the spells in the game. But each game during the drafting stage make a conscious note of whether there are any spells on the enemy team that will ruin your day even after you have those items. If you identify such abilities — always stay on the fringes of team fights until you see them being used up and then go in.

Extreme survivability. This build is aimed as a learning experience. You will feel how steep it is to buy items with big, expensive components as early as your 2nd choice. On the other hand, you will experience a drastic difference in your survivability, since every angle is covered — magic resistance, evasion, debuff immunity, lifesteal, 2x self-dispels, and CC on demand with Abyssal Blade. With experience, you will eventually develop a feel for which items to incorporate into your standard builds and when.
Situational Items
Buy after Manta, or later, to counter a high-evasion hero.
Buy after Manta to counter enemy buffs which prevent you from killing your targets — ex. Ghost Scepter, Glimmer Cape, Eul's Scepter, Aeon Disk, etc.
Buy as your 3rd item at the earliest, when facing hard CC spells that Counterspell cannot deal with.
Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
Blink on top of an enemy hero, use Manta to summon illusions, burn their mana, and finish them off with Mana Void.
Always Blink away from a fight if you are about to die. You are useless when dead, but extremely powerful when alive.
Farm the jungle between waves to maximize your gold income. Never stand idle.
Use reactively to reflect single-target spells — such as Sven's or Vengeful Spirit's — back at the caster.
Blink through terrain to escape ganks. You can pass through cliffs and trees, not just between units.
Anti-Mage has the fastest Base Attack Time in the game. This means that bonuses to Attack Speed & Agility are even more effective on him than on most other heroes. You can learn more about this in our Base Attack Time concept page.
