Overview
Lotus Orb is strongest against spells and items that can be both reflected and dispelled. You choose which effect to prioritize, and even if you're too slow to reflect it, you can still dispel it afterwards. It also gives a large armor bonus plus extra mana and mana regeneration, which makes it a great buy for heroes with mana problems who also get focused by physical damage.

Advanced Tip
If the enemy caster's spell is upgraded by Aghanim's Scepter or Aghanim's Shard, Lotus Orb reflects the upgraded version. But if the upgrade transforms an otherwise targeted spell into an AoE one, then it will be no longer reflected at all - ex. Storm Spirit's Electric Vortex.
