Carry · Mobile · Assassin · Split-pusher

Strengths

  • Very mobile hero
  • Counters high mana pool heroes
  • Can reflect spells with Counterspell

Weaknesses

  • Weak laning stage
  • Low health & armor
  • Most late game carries outscale him
Game Plan

Early Game

Farm defensively & only join good fights with low to no risk of dying.

Mid Game

Split push lanes while your team applies pressure. Force enemies to react to you.

Late Game

Pick off isolated heroes with Blink. Use Mana Void to punish high-mana enemies in teamfights.

Extras

Can solo Roshan and/or Tormentor once sufficiently farmed. Use this to gain Aegis advantage before pushing.

Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
Blink on top of an enemy hero, use Manta to summon illusions, burn his mana and finish him off with Mana Void.
Always Blink away from a fight if you're about to die. You're useless dead but extremely powerful alive.
Farm the jungle between waves to maximize your gold income. Never stand idle.
Use Counterspell reactively to reflect single-target spells like Lion's Hex or Lina's Laguna Blade back at the caster.
Blink through terrain to escape ganks — you can pass through cliffs and trees, not just between units.
Split push two lanes simultaneously in late game to create an impossible dilemma for the enemy team: defend both or lose buildings.
