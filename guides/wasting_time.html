You're Wasting 10-15 Minutes Every Game Without Realizing It

Most players never put their finger on this one - so it feels inconsequential. But once you see it, you can't unsee it. And fixing it is one of the highest-value things you can do for your game.

We all love DotA for a multitude of reasons. Some find the thrill in the competitive nature of the game. Others love being the mastermind whose plan comes together. But one thing remains true - there are near-endless building blocks to this game. And most players only ever focus on a few, because identifying what areas you need to self-improve at, and how, is an entire skill of its own. So let me help you with that.

The Problem

DotA is a game of near-infinite, constant decision-making. And 99% of players waste time while doing it. A few small examples. You just finished a big teamfight and you're thinking what to do next - keep pushing, go back to heal, go farm the jungle? Five seconds later you finally decide and proceed. Or you've just respawned. You look at the minimap, manually check a few areas to see what's going on, and ten seconds later you decide to TP somewhere based on the gathered info.

And then your forty-minute game ends. If you go back to your replay and focus on all the inefficiencies, you'll be shocked to realize that you've wasted probably around ten to fifteen full minutes doing completely nothing in-game.

Imagine going back and playing the same game again - but the other nine players start at the ten-minute mark. Imagine how insane that sounds. Yet it's pretty close to reality.

Everyone is playing like this. And the difference between the best players in the world and the worst ones is simply the amount of time wasted. The best ones waste around one to two minutes in a normal game. The average player wastes around half their entire match.

The Fix

What would someone pointing out a problem be, without providing a solution? Nothing different than simple complaining. So let's fix it.

When you spend time thinking about your next actions - that's not wasted time. You're being mindful about your gameplay, and that's mandatory. The inefficiency is when you do it during a game while not actually doing anything at the same time. There are two main ways to fix this.

01
Have a Pre-Game Plan
Answer the big questions before you queue. What you're playing, where you're going, what you're building. Eliminate the thinking that doesn't need to happen in-game.
02
Plan Ahead
Start thinking about your next action before your current one is finished. By the time the teamfight ends, you should already know where you're going next.

A perfect player would waste zero seconds even in a three-hour match, if they do both of these things correctly.

The Pre-Game Plan

By having a pre-game plan, you eliminate 90% of the things most players think about during a game. Which skill should I pick? What talent to choose? What items to buy? Where to go and when? Those questions - and many others - can be pre-answered before you even queue into a match. And almost nobody does this. Not because it's too hard, but because it takes extra effort and time outside the game.

But if you cover the macro questions beforehand, you now have all of that free time during the game to actually play it - focusing on the micro aspects that are otherwise buried under the macro ones. You can proactively make plays on the map instead of just reacting mindlessly to the enemy. You can focus on objectives more clearly. You execute mechanical things more proficiently - because you're not busy thinking about ten different things while trying to last hit a catapult under your own tower.

This is just one of the numerous concepts that inhabit our beloved game. Let's keep exploring them together.
