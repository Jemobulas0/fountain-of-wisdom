Overview
Break is a situational but extremely powerful debuff which you can apply to an enemy hero. There are only a few sources of Break in the entire game. Silver Edge is the go-to option for heroes who focus more on auto attacks rather than spells. Regardless if you're an assassin who naturally buys it or a hero who doesn't have it in his usual builds - if the team needs a Break, consider it.
Use Cases
Break: Silence your target's Passive Abilities for 5 seconds. The effect is Undispellable, but blockable by Debuff Immunity.
Kill Threat: Your first hit breaks you out of invisibility, deals 300 bonus damage & caps your target's movement speed to 200 for 5 seconds. This means that no amount of movement speed buffs can raise it above 200 and your victim is effectively dead in the water if he doesn't have an escape like Blink.
Utility: Having a 17 seconds invisibility with +20% movement speed allows you to both escape and join fights faster, or simply traverse the map more efficiently. Also having only 23s cooldown means that you have effectively only a 6s downtime of not being invisible when you're not being forced to break out of it.
Strong Against
You want to counter strong passive abilities which make certain heroes nigh-unkillable. Next best thing is to remove strong passives which significantly boost damage output. And in the case of a few heroes - you do both. Abaddon is a unique case - he can still manually press his ultimate even when stunned - so you have to combine your Break with Silence to delete him properly.
Natural Buyers
Silver Edge provides a ton of bonus dmg plus a decent amount of attack speed. Which makes carry heroes - especially ones with bonus effects to their auto attacks - a great choice to buy it.
