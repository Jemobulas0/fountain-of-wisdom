Overview
Branch provides the best stats-per-gold ratio in the game for early game trading. It's a great inventory filler for your starting items and it has a number of unique uses.
Use Cases
You can consume the planted Tree with a
Tango in order to double the duration and thus the overall healing — from 112 to 224 HP.
You can use the planted Tree to block vision from the enemy — preventing Targeted Abilities from being cast on you and stopping auto attacks. A great trick in the early game if you're getting dove under your own tower, buying you a couple of seconds during which you might even turn the fight around and get the kill yourself.
You can block narrow paths and chokepoints. Especially easy to pull off when running through tight forest paths.
When the Tree spawns, it pushes units away from its center. This can be used to unstuck yourself from cliffs, or to push down an enemy hero hiding on them. But most notably — you can counter certain enemy spells this way. For example, you can push allies caught at the edge of
Chronosphere and other similar spells outside of their range, freeing your ally. And you can also break the leash from
Slark's
Pounce.
