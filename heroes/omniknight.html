Game Plan

Early Game

Focus on trading frequently with Hammer of Purity & backing off immediately if they have high kill potential. Wear them down. And when they're finally open after you've exhausted their sustain — go for the kills!

Mid Game

You're a powerhouse. The enemy team most likely prefers to be on the other side of the map. If possible, play aggressive on the map & force objectives whenever your Guardian Angel is up.

Late Game

Group up with your team & deathball into the enemies. Your spells are amazingly strong, but only for a short while. In prolonged fights where you're either getting kited or outsustained — you're at a disadvantage.

Extras

Your preferred playstyle when you're at your strongest is to be aggressive on the map. This isn't possible every single game be it because the enemy team isn't allowing you or your team is simply too passive. In those games it's better to play the long game instead of griefing yourself by being overly aggressive.

Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
Purification heals an ally while dealing the same value as pure damage to nearby enemies. Always try to cast it when you can tag at least one enemy — you heal your ally and damage them at the same time.
Repel grants complete spell immunity. Use it proactively before going into a fight, not reactively when you're already being destroyed.
Guardian Angel only blocks physical damage. Enemies with heavy magic damage will still hurt your team through it — keep that in mind before committing.
Your greatest enemy is silence. An enemy team with multiple silence sources will completely shut you down. Identify this during the draft & plan accordingly — BKB or well-timed Repel on yourself are your answers.
You can cast Guardian Angel on a single ally instead of your whole team. If you see a carry about to die in a side fight, a targeted cast can save the game without burning your cooldown on the whole team.
Hammer of Purity applies a strong slow. Use this to repeatedly stick to an enemy target in lane — each slow gives you another free hit before they can create distance.
