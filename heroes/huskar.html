Game Plan

Early Game

Annihilate your lane. Freeze the creep equilibrium in front of your tower. Position yourself forward (around the enemy ranged creep) and W-click the enemy hero when he comes close in order to avoid drawing creep aggro thus ruining your equilibrium & losing unnecessary HP. As late as level 3 you should be able to pull off without an issue. If done properly, your opponent should be forced to abandon the lane or do long range farming if his spell kit allows it.

Mid Game

There's almost no other hero who wants to be as aggressive as Huskar does so early on. Identify if there are any heroes on the enemy team who can actually counter you hard (ex. AA's Ice Blast) & play around them. Everyone else — run them down & take early objectives with your team. If you manage to jump a hero who has no mobility — it's almost always a guaranteed kill straight away.

Late Game

At this point in the game the enemy team is more grouped up & geared up. So even if you were doing amazing up until now, you should go down a gear and play more around your team. Hard disable a key enemy hero with your Agh's, create as much chaos in the enemy midst and even if you go down, make sure that most of the enemy team goes down with you.

Extras

You can actually kill Roshan as early as level 7 once you buy your Armlet. My personal record in a real game is to kill him at 6:30 on lvl 5. Make sure to always buy your own Smoke & go into the Rosh pit that way — never go in without it. Works even better if the enemy team has an early Obs & gets a false sense of security by not seeing you.

Inner Fire not only Silences all enemies in AoE, but it also applies a Knockback effect. This means that all sorts of mobility spells get instantly cancelled out & the enemy hero gets stopped & knocked back: Techies' Blast Off!; Mirana's Leap; Spirit Breaker's Charge; Magnus' Skewer, etc.
Your Shard grants you a new active ability, which is an extra Basic Self-Dispel. It costs 30% of Current HP & 3 seconds later it heals you back for the amount of health consumed + 3% Max HP per debuff dispelled. This means that you can use the spell in several different ways. One is to simply wait for a specific debuff that you want to dispel. Another is if you're worried about getting killed quickly once you go in, you could activate the ability at Full HP before engaging (thus sacrificing the maximum possible amount of HP), jump into the fight & take a lot of damage initially, but then 3 seconds later you'll receive a large heal — thus surviving their initial burst & hopefully winning the fight as a result. The drawback is that your Life Break will also do less damage since your HP will be lower. The second use keeps the ability relevant even when you're not trying to actively dispel something.
All of your Abilities & Items cost Health instead of Mana. This Health cost is applied to you in the form of Magical Damage. This means that any sources of Magic Resistance will actually reduce your Health costs. It's far from mandatory, but asking a teammate (most likely your offlaner) to buy a
at some point during the game could be highly beneficial to you. The active effect will also protect you from your own self-afflicted damage from Life Break.
The ability which defines Huskar is Berserker's Blood. The lower his Max HP is, the higher his Offense & Defense capabilities are. Since he already gains immense amounts of Attack Speed at Low HP & higher HP Regen based on his Strength, it's only natural to aim for Strength & Damage items, while buying the necessary survivability while running headfirst into danger.
During Life Break's leap duration you're Debuff Immune & upon crashing into your target you apply Basic Dispel to yourself. In certain scenarios you'll want to Blink next to an enemy, wait for him to hit you with a nasty debuff like Spirit Vessel & then immediately Life Break. You can also use items & spells while flying with your ultimate.

Use the Armlet Toggle technique to effectively become immortal. This is how you can kill Rosh in the early game & this is how you can fight certain enemy heroes without them being able to ever kill you in the early & mid game. It's even possible to Armlet Toggle against many DoTs in the game — some tic multiple times per second making it almost impossible, but others tic much slower thus allowing you to avoid lethal damage. Same logic applies when fighting heroes with fast & slow attack speed.
Specifically for Roshan — don't stress too much about that if you're new to the hero. Get a couple of games under your belt first. Then go into Demo & practice until you get it right. Once you get the hang of it, it will become second nature and you won't accidentally kill yourself in a real game, thus ruining your entire early game & potentially the whole match.
This hero finds his greatest success on the edge of aggression. The more that you push your limits, the better you will do with Huskar. But this isn't an excuse to just feed. If you go past your limits, you become a detriment to your team. Many people pick certain heroes, because it's easy to attribute a larger amount of deaths to the hero or the role. In reality this is absolutely never true in any scenario & it's just a toxic excuse. Same rule applies here. Practice, find your limits & play on the razor's edge!
