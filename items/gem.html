Build Tree
Gem of True Sight
900
Overview
A risky purchase with a huge payoff. You see all invisible enemy units and wards in a large radius around you, and you can reveal wards and enemies hidden in the fog on nearby cliffs or in trees. The cost is that the Gem drops when you die, and the enemy team can pick it up for free.
Counters
Invisible heroes and items the Gem exposes.
Natural Buyers
The Frontliner
Beefy heroes survive fights more often, and they stand closest to where invisible enemies try to get in. The Gem is safest on the hero least likely to drop it.
The Self-Sufficient Support
Supports with escape or survivability tools and spare gold can carry the Gem themselves, so the cores keep their item slots for damage.
The Map Controller
Mobile supports with a lot of downtime between fights can walk the map and clear enemy wards from large areas you care about, such as Roshan or the jungle you want to farm. The faster the hero moves around the map, the more ground the Gem covers.
The Split Pusher
If your main job is to split push for most of the game, but there's an invisible hero on the enemy team who can kill you every time he catches you off guard, the Gem shows him long before he reaches you. He needs to get close, so you almost always have time to leave.
