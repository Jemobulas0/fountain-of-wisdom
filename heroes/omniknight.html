Game Plan

Early Game

Focus on trading frequently with Hammer of Purity & backing off immediately if they have high kill potential. Wear them down. And when they're finally open after you've exhausted their sustain — go for the kills!

Mid Game

You're a powerhouse. The enemy team most likely prefers to be on the other side of the map. If possible, play aggressive on the map & force objectives whenever your Guardian Angel is up.

Late Game

Group up with your team & deathball into the enemies. Your spells are amazingly strong, but only for a short while. In prolonged fights where you're either getting kited or outsustained — you're at a disadvantage.

Extras

Your preferred playstyle when you're at your strongest is to be aggressive on the map. This isn't possible every single game be it because the enemy team isn't allowing you or your team is simply too passive. In those games it's better to play the long game instead of griefing yourself by being overly aggressive.

Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
Purification & Hammer of Purity deal Pure Damage. This is especially powerful against Low HP & High Armor/Magic Resistance enemies (ex. Terrorblade). Also Hammer of Purity costs no Mana. Spam it off cooldown in the lane for maximum harass.
Repel provides primarily Magic Resistance & Debuff Immunity. This means that you have to approach different heroes differently. When running into stuns or silences, you have to cast it in advance. But when facing soft CCs like slows & roots, you can wait until you get hit by them & only then Repel yourself, as to not lose any of the spell's duration — or give an opportunity to your enemies to just wait it out & then CC you.
Your ultimate is completely undispellable & it will protect your allies as long as you're near them. The radius is decent, but still be mindful that you're not too far away when a teammate gets focused down.
Your Scepter turns Guardian Angel into a global spell, which affects buildings & doubles Health Restoration. This effectively gives your team an extra Glyph of Fortification. And it makes Omniknight a great synergy hero with other healers. And naturally — if you're about to ult with a Scepter, save your Purification for after using Guardian Angel.
The later the game goes on, often times the more important it is to cast Repel on your carry or midlaner. Be mindful of who's the higher priority hero on your team to receive the spell. With that in mind, if you have an Enigma who wants to Blink in with Black Hole & has no BKB (or a similar support) — don't be shy from casting Repel on him as well.
Your Innate Ability is a short range brutal slow aura. It is extremely uncomfortable for enemy melee heroes to run around you in the battlefield without using items or spells just for that. So get in their faces & bring down the hammer!
