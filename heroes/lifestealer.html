Game Plan

Early Game

Dominate your lane & hold it longer than most other carries.

Mid Game

Ideally you want to start farming the enemy Ancients when you get your first big item. Reason being that you can win most fights even 1v2 if you were doing well up to this point. And if they bring most of their team, you just run away & create space on the map for yours. On the other hand if you aren't that strong, then you can rotate to your own Ancients & farm them and your own offlane.

Late Game

If there are no Debuff Immunity Piercing CCs, you can front line for your team. Otherwise identify the thing/s which can take you out before you have a chance to respond & commit only after they've been used up.

Extras

Hitting level 6 quickly & using your first couple of Infests on certain Ancient Creeps in order to slam yourself at enemy heroes with 1-2 extra allies is an incredibly easy way to get early kills.

Infesting allied initiators allows you to have instant impact & be brought directly in your enemies' face, which most other carries only dream of. (ex. Legion Commander, Tidehunter, Spirit Breaker, Storm Spirit)

Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
You stack bonus max HP indefinitely — even when denying allied creeps & heroes.
Feast allows you to melt high HP targets which makes bonuses to your Attack Speed all the better.
Rage is your own personal & improved BKB with a Movement Speed boost.
Aghanim's Scepter allows you to go in, get focused with certain big spells (ex. Troll Warlord's Battle Trance) & Infest an enemy hero to both deal damage, heal back up, wait out for the danger to go away & have your spells partially recharge.
You can use Infest to greatly heal an ally hero for up to 1200 HP instantly in a fight and either pop-out instantly for AoE dmg & keep fighting, or stay inside to give your ally bonus Movement Speed for you both to run away from danger.
You have 3 main purposes for your Infest besides the early game Ancient creep kill potential: Go in with your Initiator, Heal an ally in fights or Infest an enemy hero with Aghanim's. Always have in mind in advance which would be your best options in fights.
Once you have & : using → → actually gives you enough time of Debuff Immunity + Invulnerability for your Rage to fully recharge & use it a 2nd time without becoming susceptible to normal spells. And once the 2nd Rage expires, you only have 7 seconds until your second Infest is back up again.
