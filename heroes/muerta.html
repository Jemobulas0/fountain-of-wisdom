Game Plan

Early Game

You have the ability to delete most offlaners as early as level 2, if your support has a high kill threat potential. Do not overextend though as you're extremely squishy early on. Focus on hitting your first farming item as soon as possible in order to not fall behind the enemy carry too much.

Mid Game

Unlike some other heroes, you can't just chill in the jungle and hope for the best. You're decent at farming, but far from being the best. Utilize your extreme kill threat potential by farming & playing around your team — especially when your ultimate is up. Look for opportunities to delete Roshan with Pierce the Veil & the Tormentor with that + BKB.

Late Game

If you succeed in spending even a couple of seconds of free-firing at the enemy team without being disrupted, you'll most likely win your teamfights. Your itemization is extremely important each game — as is your positioning & timing in fights. Your sustain damage is nice, but what's truly important is to apply your huge burst at the right time & on the right target.

Extras

Your Innate allows you to stack Spell Amplification to extreme numbers. And even though you have a limit, you lose half of your stacks rounded down (currently this is bugged and they're being sadly rounded up). So even though it's in your best interest to not die, do not fall into the psychological trap of playing too passively & losing the game because of this. At the end of the day, your goal is the destruction of the enemy Throne — not to accumulate as many stacks as possible.
