Lore by Jemobulas
After creating the imperfect Chains of Abscession, which led to the destruction of her homeland, she vowed to atone for her failure. And thus she poured all of herself into her ultimate creation, in order to have her vengeance on the Dead God!
Overview
Do you want to completely deny almost the entire Health Restoration of all enemies around you, while also stealing it for yourself, while also stealing their movement speed, while also being completely immune to physical damage, while also melting everyone around you with your awesome AoE capabilities? Then this item is for you!

Strong Against
The most notable heroes & items the Crozier counters.
Natural Buyers
Heroes who like to run into the middle of the enemy team while dealing massive AoE spell damage, since being Ethereal disables only your auto attacks. On top of that, if you're affecting enemy heroes who are attempting to restore massive amounts of HP from any source, like strong heals, lifesteal, or massive health regen such as Alchemist's Chemical Rage or Heart of Tarrasque, not only will you deny them said healing, but you'll be the one to receive it instead, making you virtually unkillable despite your aggressive positioning. And the cherry on top is that you usually buy this item later into the game. So if the enemy hero or heroes you're targeting don't already have BKB, it will be extremely awkward for them to buy one at this point. Or at the very least, it will already be brought down to its minimum duration.
Tips
  1. The only hard counter to Crella's Crozier is Debuff Immunity, like BKB or Omniknight's Repel.
  2. Each pulse only affects enemies within the 900 radius at the moment it goes off. Enemies who walk out of range avoid the rest of the pulses.
  3. A dispel removes your ghost form and your stolen movement speed, but not the healing drain or the stealing of movement speed. Under a continuous dispel like Nullifier, you still get to use each stolen speed stack for about half a second.
  4. Both positive and negative Health Restoration applied to you affect how much you heal when stealing the enemies' Health Restoration around you.
  5. Combine it with BKB to avoid the self-inflicted magic amplification, at the cost of your physical damage immunity. Your healing and movement speed steal keep working.
  6. The aura redirects nothing from enemies at full health.
  7. Never buy two on one team! Due to a bug, if both Croziers are affecting an enemy but only one of them is activated, the -90% Health Restoration never gets applied, because the passive aura effect of -30% overrides it completely. On top of all that, if only one is activated, the Health Restoration redirection bugs out as well and you get 0 healing from it. And if both are active, only the player who activates his Crozier first gets the healing, while the other one remains bugged.
