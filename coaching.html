One-on-One Coaching

Master the Game.
Define the Meta.

23 Years Playing DotA. 20 Years Coaching. Peaked at 10k+.

Give a player a build and he wins a game.
Teach him to understand the game and the patch will never matter again.

01
It only works if you do the work
I can hand you everything I know. It doesn't become yours until you take it into your own games and struggle with it there. Knowledge only turns into skill through practice. That part is on you, and there's no way around it.
02
You set the direction as much as I do
Some people arrive knowing exactly what they want to focus on. Others want help working out what that should be — which is part of what I'm here for. Either way, I shape the session around you.
03
The goal is for you to stop needing me as soon as possible
No packages, no subscriptions, no paywalls. Every session ends with something specific to work on, and if it lands, you'll come back to build on it — until you reach the level you set out for.
The Session
Two Ways to Work Together
Send a Replay — €20 · up to three games
You fill in a short form — your match IDs, your rank, your role, and what you want looked at. I analyze them in depth and send you a written breakdown within a week. What I'm looking for is the thing costing you the most — the weakest link in how you're playing right now — and what to do about it, in a form you can take into your next game.
Book a Live Session — €30 · 60 minutes
Voice and screen share on Discord. We start with what you're after — a single session on something specific, or the first step of a longer climb. If you already have clear objectives, we tackle them. If you don't, we set them together. The hour is yours and it goes where it needs to go.
What to Bring

You came here because you know some part of your game is lacking. Bring the games you think show it best.

A game where you got completely dismantled and have no idea why is just as useful as a highly contested one. Confusion is more useful to me than a loss or a win.

Whatever your rank, the fundamentals don't change. Only which one you're currently working through.

Is This for You
Yes
If you want to genuinely understand the game and grow as a player. Herald or Immortal, you get the same attention and the same honest read. I'm not here to judge your mistakes — they are the material we work with. We're here to learn and improve while enjoying doing it.
No
If you're after cheese and shortcuts. MMR should be a representation of your true skill, not the thing itself. And chasing the number instead of the understanding doesn't hold up. If you're coming to have your opinions confirmed rather than examined, we'll both waste the hour.
