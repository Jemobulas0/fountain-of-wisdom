Game Plan

Early Game

You have one of the strongest normal spells in the entire game. Blade Fury does 400 AoE Damage on level 1. If you have a high kill threat support on your lane, you can immediately kill someone on lane before even a single creep has fallen. You're a lane dominator — so dominate!

Mid Game

The moment you hit Battle Fury, you immediately switch to max farming mode in between your ultimates. But don't just mindlessly rush at enemy heroes when Omnislash is up. Farm well, play smart & look for good opportunities to fight.

Healing Ward is an extremely powerful AoE healing ability. Don't go on for minutes without using it. First — it allows you to take Tormentor & Roshan much quicker than most normal team drafts are able to. And second — even if you're simply jungling for a while & you see a teammate being low on HP on his lane — just pop the spell & send it to him. It lasts for 24 seconds. This is more than enough time to reach him & heal him up, without you wasting any time to go there yourself.

Late Game

You have two main ways of approaching the late game definitive team fights. You either clump into a death ball with your team or you wait on the side to either follow up on your initiator or counter-initiate theirs. This depends on the enemy team — if they want you grouped up together for a Shaker, Puck or Enigma ults for example, then you want to be on the side & look for your opening to jump in. If you get caught in such spells, you'll just die for free. But if no such threat exists — just pop that Healing Ward, walk high ground with your team & force the enemy team to fight you on your own terms, without you overextending yourself.

Extras

Omnislash allows you to be one of the few heroes who can straight up kill a hero from full to zero. But it's also one of the easiest spells to counter with a large number of items & spells or simply by walking next to a creep for you to bounce to it, and then the enemy hero walking away to safety. Glimmer Cape you can counter with cheap Dust. But things like Eul's will require more. So always try to predict in advance how things will go before Omnislashing.

Tips & Tricks
Basic
Advanced
Blade Fury is both your best offensive & defensive ability for the early parts of the game. Be mindful how & when you're using it.
Cast Healing Ward before going into a fight. Not when people are already low.
Omnislash's number of hits is based on your Attack Speed. & all on-hit effects also work fully during your ultimate.
During Blade Fury you can still proc on-hit effects on units and also deal normal damage to non-units like buildings, wards & Phoenix's or KotL's .
In fights you can allow yourself to get hit by soft CCs (not silence or stun) and cast right after — thus wasting the spells of your enemies.
In the late game prioritize 's defensive qualities. As early as your first big item, your auto attacks are already dealing higher DPS than the spell. But keep in mind that the spell is a double-edged blade. In a scenario where you're fighting an enemy hero and you can kill him, you might decide to spin & die, because you've lost a lot of your DPS.
Attempt to micro your — especially in the early parts of the game. The reason it provides amazing healing is that it's also quite easy to be taken out.
Your level 25 talent allows you to basically heal to full every time you cast . Keep in mind that the ability also applies a Basic Dispel to yourself just like your ultimate does — even though it's not stated in the tooltip. So unless you need it in order to reach an enemy, save it for after a fight starts & you've lost some health first.
