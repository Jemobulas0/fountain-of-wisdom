I've played DotA since before eSports existed. I've spent more hours in this game than most people spend on any single thing in their lives — and what I found is that almost everything commonly taught about improvement is either wrong, incomplete, or aimed at the wrong problem.

I don't teach you builds to copy. I teach you how to think about the game. My students don't just climb — they understand why they're climbing. That's the difference between a rank that reflects your actual skill and one that doesn't.
